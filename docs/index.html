What the data can and can't tell you

  • Formal organizations. It counts registered nonprofit organizations and the opportunities they describe. Civic life that runs through informal groups, schools, or local government isn't captured.
  • Based on tax returns and websites. An organization that doesn't describe an opportunity in its IRS tax return or on a website counts as not offering it.
  • One snapshot. It shows how things look at one point in time, not change or impact.
  • No names. Records are de-identified, so the site shows kinds of organizations, never specific ones.
  • Some types are unknown. About 4 in 10 organizations offering opportunities have no identified type.
