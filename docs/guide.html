How to use this guide

Bring it to a meeting, class, or community gathering. It moves in six short steps. The data describes what formal nonprofit organizations describe in their IRS tax returns and websites; the people in the room know the rest, so give most of the time to talking. If someone answers in a word or two, ask: "Why do you think that?"

Step 1 · Shared meaning

What does "getting involved" mean to you?

Go around the room and let everyone answer in their own words. Then notice together: what do the answers have in common, and where are they very different?

Step 2 · Guess first

Before looking at the data

Ask everyone to write down a guess. Keep the answers for step 3.
