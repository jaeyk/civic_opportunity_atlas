Step 1 · Look

What we found

    How these numbers are made
    1. Start with every nonprofit. All tax-exempt organizations registered with the IRS that have a mailing address here. Organizations that use a P.O. box are left out, because where they work is uncertain.
    2. Read their tax returns and websites. Each organization's IRS tax return and website are checked for four kinds of opportunity: membership, volunteering, public events, and civic or political action. An opportunity not described in either counts as "not listed".
    3. Sort them into kinds. A computer model, trained on more than 9,000 organizations that people labeled by hand, assigns one of 15 kinds. It is right about 9 times in 10; when it can't tell, the organization shows as "Type not identified".
    4. Count and compare. Local chapters of the same network count once. Counts are turned into rates per 10,000 residents, small places are smoothed, and each place is set beside the 10 places most like it.

    Full details are in the data paper and About the data.
